# FireTrace — Wildfire Forensic Analysis

## Credits

- **Interface concept and design:** inspired by the *FireTrace* prototype by **Dilum Sanjaya**, shown in a video on X (Oct 2026). His code was not used.
- **Data analysis — Rui Mendes:**
  - collected and cross-checked official reports (CTI, ADAI), ICNF/SGIF records and satellite data;
  - reconstructed the fire progression and validated it against the official burned area (ICNF vs Sentinel-2: 91% overlap);
  - digitized ANPC resource data and geolocated fatalities, command posts and road closures.
- **Code:** generated by AI (Claude Code, Anthropic) under Rui Mendes's direction, using three.js and Python (rasterio, pandas, scipy).

The site shows the Pedrógão Grande 2017 reconstruction (real data). It opens with a notice listing sources and limitations; you can reopen it by clicking the logo or via *Share → Sources, limitations & credits*.


An interactive three.js reconstruction of the Pedrógão Grande + Góis fires (17–22 June 2017), inspired by the FireTrace concept video. Terrain, imagery, burned area, fire progression, casualties and resources come from real data; the drones, aircraft and vehicle movements are illustrative.

## Run

```bash
cd /Users/ruimendes/firetrace-pedrogao && python3 -m http.server 5178
```

Open http://localhost:5178. It needs an internet connection for three.js (unpkg) and the fonts.

## What's in it

**Top bar**: incident menu, date-range menu (jump to key moments of 17–22 June), the Incident / Drones / Resources modes with badges, View, Analysis and Share menus, and a settings dialog.

**Incident mode**
- 3D terrain over a 50 × 50 km area (Copernicus DEM) with Sentinel-2 imagery, baked hillshade and cast shadows, real relief out to the horizon, OSM roads and villages, and about 200k trees placed from NDVI.
- Fire progression is digitized from the official ADAI/CEIF isochrone map and fused with VIIRS/MODIS detections. It drives the burn scar (dNBR severity), a glowing active front with embers, 6-hour isochrones, and burned or burning trees.
- The progression path has timestamp markers, plus modeled spread arrows, an ignition marker with a pulsing ignition-probability zone, the final perimeter (dashed), retardant drops that fade over the following days, and fire line.
- Layers panel with checkboxes and opacity sliders for all 10 layers, including animated wind vectors.
- The Analysis, Evidence and Notes tabs work. Notes are saved locally. You can edit the Incident Overview. Weather & Fire Behavior can read at the cursor, the fire front or the ignition point.
- Hovering shows elevation, slope, aspect, inside or outside the perimeter, burn time, fuel and lat/lon.
- 2D/3D toggle switches to an animated topo map with contours, a lat/lon grid and edge labels.
- Map controls: compass (click to reset north), dynamic scale bar, zoom presets and fullscreen.
- Timeline: play, pause, next-event, speed presets, scrubbing, event markers and an activity band. Charts show fire area, rate of spread and the elevation profile, solid for the past and dashed for the future. Click a chart to scrub. Each chart's ⋮ menu exports CSV.
- Key Events: click one to jump the time and camera there. Use + Add to create a custom event.
- **Analysis dashboard** (Analysis → Analysis dashboard, or key D): KPIs and charts with hover readouts.
  - Charts: burned area against the official ICNF values, growth rate, spread speed, ERA5 temperature/humidity/wind, the Hot-Dry-Windy index, the June FWI computed from ERA5 against the SGIF value, FIRMS radiative power, and fatalities against fire arrival.
  - Breakdowns: burn severity, land cover burned, terrain, and municipalities (click one to fly there).
  - Validation table against the CTI fatality sites.
- Each bottom chart can show any of these metrics: click its title. There is also a municipal boundaries layer.

**Drones mode**: six drones (FT-01 to FT-06), each with its own flight pattern: perimeter mapping, head-fire orbit, spot-fire orbit, structure patrol, survey lines and comms relay. The live camera feed has these controls:
- EO/IR toggle, with Ironbow or White-hot palettes
- Zoom
- Snapshot, which saves a still to Evidence
- Full camera view, plus close
- A HUD showing heading tape, gimbal scale, telemetry, SPI coordinates and a hot-spot box read back from the thermal render

The fleet list and detail card update live. Altitude, max-temperature and battery charts cover the last 60 s, and the Detections feed lists hot spots.

**Resources mode**: 13 units, all live:
- Six aircraft fly inbound, on final, dropping and returning. They release retardant or water particles and leave strips on the ground.
- Engines drive the roads.
- Crews and a dozer build and patrol fire line.

The panel shows the Air Ops Log, summary stats and the retardant, line and personnel charts. Click a unit for Chase, Cockpit or Overview (Ride along for ground units). In the camera: drag to look around, scroll to change distance, and press C to switch view.

**Keyboard**
- Space: play/pause
- ← / →: step 1 h (hold Shift for 6 h)
- 2 / 3: 2D / 3D
- C: camera view
- M: measure
- L: labels
- R: reset camera
- F: fullscreen
- Esc: back out

**Share**: copy a link to the current view (time, mode and camera are kept in the URL hash), export a PNG snapshot, or open a printable report.
